# Test drive procedure

> **Synthetic content.** Fictional dealer, patterned on UAE retail practice.

## Slot availability

Test drives are booked in **2-hour slots**. The 2 hours covers a short
briefing at the desk, the drive itself (typically 40–60 minutes on a
standard route with the option to extend), time for questions afterwards,
and a chance to sit with a consultant to talk through financing or
trade-in if the customer wants to.

Available slots by day:

| Day | First slot | Last slot |
|---|---|---|
| Monday – Thursday | 09:30 | 20:00 |
| Friday | 14:30 | 20:30 |
| Saturday | 10:30 | 20:30 |
| Sunday | 10:30 | 19:30 |

**Saturday is our busiest day.** Slots between 15:00 and 20:00 on Saturday
book out three to five days in advance. Customers asking for a Saturday
afternoon at short notice should be offered the nearest alternative rather
than told the day is unavailable.

**Friday afternoons are the second-busiest** slot for weekend buyers.

## Booking channels

Bookings come through three channels:

1. **Website form or WhatsApp** — the assistant on this platform can hold a
   provisional slot but not confirm one. Confirmation happens when a
   consultant calls or messages back within one hour during showroom hours.
2. **In person at the showroom** — a walk-in customer can be booked for a
   slot the same day if one is free.
3. **Phone** — the sales line during showroom hours.

## What we need to confirm a slot

Every test-drive booking needs three things:

1. **Which vehicle.** Brand and model at minimum. Year and trim help the
   consultant stage the right demonstrator. If we do not have the exact
   model in the demonstrator fleet, the customer is told so and offered the
   nearest available equivalent.
2. **Preferred date.** A day, not a range. A time is helpful but not
   required — if the customer does not specify a time, we offer the
   earliest slot on the requested day.
3. **A contact number** where we can call or message to confirm.

If any of these three are missing at enquiry, the assistant asks the
customer for the missing one — never all three at once. One question per
turn.

## Eligibility

Drivers must be at least **21 years old** and hold a valid driving licence
recognised in Velmora. For Karva vehicles this is the only requirement.

For **Renzo performance models** — anything the catalogue lists in the
"High-Performance", "Exotic" or "Factory Tuner" categories — the driver
must be at least **25 years old** and must have held their licence for at
least **three years**. This reflects the insurance terms attached to those
vehicles, not a judgement about the driver.

## Documents to bring

On the day of the test drive:

1. **Original Emirates ID** (or passport plus residence visa for
   expatriates on a visit visa).
2. **Original driving licence** — a photograph on a phone is not enough.
3. Nothing else is required for the drive itself.

The consultant photographs the licence at the desk before the drive begins.
The photograph is retained for 90 days and is used only for insurance and
compliance records.

## Accompanied vs unaccompanied drives

**All first test drives are accompanied** by a member of the sales team.
This is not because we distrust the driver — it is because the consultant
explains the vehicle's features during the drive, and this is often the
most useful part of the whole visit.

**Unaccompanied drives** are available on request in two cases:

1. **Returning customers** who have previously purchased from Alto Motors,
   and only for Karva models.
2. **Extended evaluations** — see below.

An unaccompanied drive requires a signed additional-driver form and a copy
of the licence held on file.

## Extended and overnight drives

A customer seriously evaluating a purchase can request:

- **An extended drive** of up to three hours, on any model.
- **An overnight loan** of a Renzo model, subject to manager approval.

Both require a **refundable security deposit** of AED 2,000 (for extended)
or AED 5,000 (for overnight), and both are always routed to the Sales
department manager for approval — the assistant does not confirm these.

## Insurance during the drive

The demonstrator vehicle is fully insured comprehensively. In the event of
an accident during a test drive:

- If the driver has followed the agreed route and traffic law, there is no
  cost to the customer.
- If the driver has driven recklessly, driven under the influence, or has
  taken the vehicle outside the agreed route without permission, the
  customer becomes liable for a deductible of up to AED 5,000 and any
  additional charges the insurer applies.

The consultant explains this at the desk before the drive begins and the
customer signs an acknowledgement.

## The route

The standard test-drive route is a 12-kilometre loop that includes:

- A slow-speed showroom-lot section (feel of the steering, mirrors,
  visibility)
- A city-street segment with typical traffic-light stops
- A short highway segment (Sheikh Zayed Road extension) to feel the car at
  cruising speed

Extended drives leave the standard route at the consultant's discretion and
may include a longer highway segment.

## Cancellations and no-shows

- **Cancellation with notice**: no penalty, at any time before the slot.
- **No-show**: the customer is contacted once to reschedule. Two no-shows
  on the same booking without contact result in a note on the customer
  record; a further booking is still accepted but is manually confirmed by
  a consultant rather than by the assistant.
- **Cancellation on the day**: no penalty, but the customer is asked to
  give at least an hour's notice so the slot can be re-offered.

## Response commitment

Test-drive enquiries are answered within **one hour** during showroom
hours. Slots are perishable — an unanswered Saturday enquiry is a lost
Saturday — so this category takes priority over financing and trade-in
enquiries when the team is at capacity.

Enquiries arriving after showroom hours are answered by 09:30 the next
working day.
