# Frequently asked questions

> **Synthetic content.** Fictional dealer, patterned on UAE retail practice.
> These are the questions we hear most often, phrased the way customers
> actually phrase them — so RAG retrieval can match the customer's own
> wording rather than the dealer's.

## Visiting and booking

**Are you open right now?**
Weekday hours are 09:00 to 21:00. Friday we open at 14:00. Saturday
10:00 to 22:00 and Sunday 10:00 to 21:00. Ramadan hours are different —
see `01-showroom-hours-and-visits.md` for the split-day schedule.

**Can I just walk in or do I need to book?**
Walk-ins are welcome any time we are open. A booking is only needed if
you want to see a specific consultant or test-drive a specific vehicle.

**Where are you located?**
Legend Motors, Showroom #46, Ras Al Khor. Parking is on the front lot.
Wheelchair access is at the main entrance.

**Do you speak Arabic?**
Yes. All customer-facing staff are bilingual (Arabic and English), and
we routinely accommodate Urdu, Hindi, Malayalam and Filipino on request.

**Can I visit today?**
If we are open, yes. See the day above. Sunday afternoons are our
quietest and best for a thorough conversation.

## Test drives

**Can I test drive on Saturday?**
Yes, but Saturday afternoons book out three to five days in advance.
Book early or ask us for the nearest available slot.

**How long is a test drive?**
The slot is 45 minutes. The actual drive is 20 to 25 minutes on a
standard 12 km route including a highway segment.

**Do I need to bring anything?**
Original Emirates ID and original driving licence. A photograph on a
phone is not enough for the licence.

**Can I drive the car alone?**
First drives are accompanied by a sales consultant, who explains the
vehicle's features. Unaccompanied drives are available for returning
customers on Karva models.

**Can I take the car overnight?**
Overnight loans are available on Renzo models with manager approval and a
refundable AED 5,000 deposit. This is not something the assistant
confirms; it goes to a sales manager.

**What if I have an accident during the test drive?**
The vehicle is fully insured. If you have followed the agreed route and
the traffic law, there is no cost. If not, a deductible up to AED 5,000
may apply. The consultant explains this at the desk before the drive.

**How old do I need to be?**
21 for Karva. 25 for Renzo performance models, and you need to have held
your licence for at least three years for those.

## Financing

**How much is the monthly instalment on a Renzo S5?**
That depends on your down payment, tenure and salary-transfer situation.
The assistant will compute an indicative instalment using the CBUAE-
mandated reducing balance method and one of our published rate bands.
Final rates and approval come from the financing bank.

**What is the minimum down payment?**
20% of the vehicle's value. This is the CBUAE regulatory floor — no bank
in our panel is allowed to offer less.

**What is the maximum tenure?**
60 months. Also a CBUAE floor. Longer terms are not available on any
UAE auto finance regardless of dealer.

**Which banks do you work with?**
Emirates NBD, ADIB, DIB, CBD, Ajman Bank, and a rotating panel of others
on request. We do not favour one — a finance consultant presents at
least three side by side.

**Do I need to transfer my salary?**
No, but every bank offers a better rate if you do — typically 100 to 200
basis points off. The assistant will quote both bands when you ask.

**Can I get Islamic finance?**
Yes. ADIB and DIB both offer Sharia-compliant Ijara and Murabaha
structures. The functional monthly cost is comparable; the legal
structure differs.

**Is there a fee for early settlement?**
Typically 1% of the outstanding balance, capped at AED 10,000 by CBUAE
rules. Some banks waive it for armed-forces personnel. The specific
bank's terms live in that bank's Key Facts Statement in our knowledge
base.

**Can I get finance if I'm on a probation period?**
Usually not on conventional bank finance — most banks want at least six
months of confirmed employment. Some Islamic banks are more flexible.
Best to ask a finance consultant with your specific profile.

**Do you finance for expatriates?**
Yes. The age at loan maturity for expatriates is 60, versus 65 for UAE
nationals, so the maximum tenure available reduces for older customers.

## Trade-in

**How much is my old car worth?**
Depends on brand, model, year, mileage and condition. The assistant can
produce an indicative range. The final offer is only made after a
physical inspection.

**Do you take any brand?**
Yes. Any passenger vehicle, up to 20 years old and 400,000 km, regardless
of brand.

**What if my car is on finance?**
No problem. We contact your bank for a settlement figure and deduct it
from the trade-in value. If the settlement exceeds the value, the
difference can often be rolled into new financing.

**Do I need an appointment?**
Weekend inspections need an appointment. Weekday walk-in inspections
between 11:00 and 17:00 are usually accepted.

**What do I bring for inspection?**
Emirates ID, original Mulkiya (not a photograph), service book if you
have it, both sets of keys, and a copy of any outstanding finance
agreement. A missing second key reduces the offer by AED 500 to 1,500;
a missing service book by 3–5% of the value.

**How long does the inspection take?**
About 45 minutes, plus time with an appraiser to review findings.

**Do I have to buy a car from you to trade in?**
No. Cash-only trade-ins are available at a slightly lower value,
reflecting the auction-only route we take with those cars.

## Delivery and paperwork

**How soon can I have the car?**
Same day if it's in stock and you're paying cash or your finance is
pre-approved. 48 hours if finance is being arranged. 2–12 weeks if
we're ordering the vehicle.

**Do you handle the RTA registration?**
Yes. Registration is done entirely by our delivery team — you don't
visit the RTA.

**Can I keep my current plate?**
Yes, plate transfer is done during registration for AED 100 (standard
series). Preferred numbers and auction plates are extra.

**Can you deliver to another emirate?**
On request, with manager approval, for a delivery fee of AED 400 to
AED 1,200 depending on distance. Not automatic — always routes to
Sales for approval.

## Warranty and service

**What warranty do I get?**
Three years or 100,000 km, whichever comes first, on every new
vehicle. Renzo also gets 24/7 roadside assistance for the warranty
period.

**When is my first service?**
At 10,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first. The labour on your
first service is on us — the voucher comes with the vehicle.

**Do I have to service with you to keep my warranty?**
No, third-party service does not automatically void the manufacturer
warranty in the UAE. But warranty claims adjacent to third-party work
get scrutinised more carefully, so most customers stick with us for
the warranty period.

**Can I book a courtesy car?**
For warranty repairs over 24 hours, yes. For scheduled service over
half a day, sometimes — usually only for Renzo customers.

## Complaints and issues

**Who do I speak to if something goes wrong?**
Customer relations. Every complaint routes there — the AI does not
attempt to handle complaints, and no complaint is answered by
automation.

**Do I have to complain in writing?**
No. In person, by phone, through the platform, or in writing all
work. Customer relations follows up in writing with whatever was
agreed.

**How long does a complaint take?**
First response within 24 hours. Resolution proposal within 5 working
days. Not closed until the customer confirms satisfaction.

## Availability

**Do you have [specific model]?**
Ask the assistant with a specific brand and model. It looks up the
catalogue directly. If the model is not in our catalogue, the honest
answer is "we don't stock that" plus the nearest equivalents we do
stock.

**When will you have [specific model] again?**
Depends on the manufacturer allocation. The sales team can answer with
a specific range once we know what you're after.

**Do you have anything in [colour]?**
Ask the assistant. Colour availability is by variant, not a general
thing.
