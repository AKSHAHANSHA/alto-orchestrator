# Showroom hours and visits

> **Synthetic content.** Fictional dealer, patterned on UAE retail practice.
> Hours below are the ones the assistant will quote if asked.

## Weekly hours

The showroom follows the standard UAE working week.

| Day | Sales & finance | Service centre |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | 09:00 – 21:00 | 08:00 – 18:00 |
| Tuesday | 09:00 – 21:00 | 08:00 – 18:00 |
| Wednesday | 09:00 – 21:00 | 08:00 – 18:00 |
| Thursday | 09:00 – 21:00 | 08:00 – 18:00 |
| Friday | 14:00 – 22:00 | 14:00 – 20:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00 – 22:00 | 08:00 – 18:00 |
| Sunday | 10:00 – 21:00 | Closed |

**Friday morning is closed** for both sales and service to observe
congregational prayer. The showroom reopens at 14:00. Customers arriving
before 14:00 on a Friday are asked to come back — we cannot let them onto
the floor early.

**Sunday is our slowest sales day** and the service centre is closed.
Sunday is the best day to visit without an appointment.

## Ramadan hours

During Ramadan the showroom shifts to a shorter, split schedule:

| Day | Sales & finance | Service centre |
|---|---|---|
| Monday – Thursday | 10:00 – 15:00 and 21:00 – 01:00 | 09:00 – 15:00 |
| Friday | Closed until 15:00, then 21:00 – 01:00 | 15:00 – 20:00 |
| Saturday | 11:00 – 15:00 and 21:00 – 01:00 | 09:00 – 15:00 |
| Sunday | 11:00 – 15:00 and 21:00 – 00:00 | Closed |

The evening slot is genuinely busy — it is when most Ramadan test drives
and paperwork completions happen. Non-Muslim customers are welcome during
the daytime slots.

Exact Ramadan dates change each year; the assistant should say
"during Ramadan" rather than quoting specific dates unless the customer
supplies them.

## Public holidays

The showroom is closed on:
- 1 January (New Year's Day)
- Eid al-Fitr (three days)
- Arafat Day and Eid al-Adha (three days)
- Islamic New Year
- Prophet's Birthday
- Commemoration Day (1 December)
- National Day (2 and 3 December)

Islamic-calendar holidays move each year; the assistant should confirm the
specific date rather than guess.

## Walk-in visits

Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. No appointment is needed for a
first visit — parking is on the front lot, entrance through the main
showroom doors, and any sales consultant on the floor can help.

If a customer wants time with a *specific* consultant they have dealt with
before, an appointment is recommended — that consultant may be with another
customer or on their day off.

Peak walk-in periods:
- **Thursday evenings** (18:00 – 21:00): highest footfall, expect a short wait.
- **Saturday all day**: consistently busy from 11:00 onwards.
- **Sunday afternoons**: quietest window, best for a thorough conversation.

## What to bring on a first visit

Nothing is required to just look at cars. If a customer wants to progress
in the same visit — to test drive, apply for finance, or start a trade-in
appraisal — they should bring:

1. **Emirates ID** (or passport plus residence visa for expatriates).
2. **A valid driving licence recognised in Velmora.** For UAE-analogue
   customers this is a UAE licence or a converted international licence.
3. **Salary certificate or bank statement (last three months)** if they
   intend to discuss financing on the same visit.

Nothing above is required simply to visit or discuss. The requirements
apply only to progressing.

## Parking

Customer parking is on the front and side lots, no charge. Handicapped
parking is at the main entrance. Service centre customers use the rear lot
and enter through the service reception.

Test-drive vehicles are staged in a separate area next to the main entrance
and are not for customer parking — they are marked with red "Demonstrator"
plates.

## Refreshments and children

Coffee, tea and water are complimentary in the showroom lounge. There is a
supervised children's area for ages 3–10 with tablets and drawing
materials; it is open only when a member of staff is present, typically
between 11:00 and 20:00.

## Language

All customer-facing staff speak Arabic and English. If a customer prefers
another language, we make a best-effort match — Urdu, Hindi, Malayalam and
Filipino are commonly spoken among our team. Requests for a specific
consultant are usually accommodated within a day.

## Accessibility

The showroom is wheelchair-accessible from the front entrance. The service
centre has a ramp at the rear entrance. Accessible parking, restrooms and
prayer facilities are on the ground floor.
