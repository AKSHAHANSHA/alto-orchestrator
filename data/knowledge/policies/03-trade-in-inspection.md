# Trade-in inspection

> **Synthetic content.** Fictional dealer, patterned on UAE retail practice.
> The valuation model is authored for this platform and is explicitly not
> based on real market data.

## What we accept

Alto Motors accepts any passenger vehicle in part exchange, **regardless of
brand**. A customer trading in a Karva or Renzo receives the same treatment
as one trading in another brand.

Vehicles are accepted up to **20 years old** and **400,000 kilometres**.
Beyond either limit, the vehicle is still considered but only after a
physical inspection — no indicative figure is given in advance, because
there is no reliable basis for one.

A trade-in is **not conditional on buying a Karva or Renzo**, though the
part-exchange value is most useful when applied against a new purchase.
Cash-only trade-ins (we buy the car, no new purchase) are available at a
lower valuation, reflecting the auction-only route we take with those
vehicles.

## Where inspection happens

All physical inspections happen at the Alto Motors showroom, at the
trade-in appraisal bay next to the service centre. Inspections are not
done at the customer's home or at a third-party location.

## Appointment vs walk-in

Inspections take **45 minutes** and are appointment-only during weekends.
Weekday walk-in inspections are accepted when an appraiser is free,
typically Monday to Thursday between 11:00 and 17:00.

Weekend inspection slots book out 24–48 hours in advance and are best
booked online or by phone.

## Documents to bring

For an inspection appointment the customer brings:

1. **Emirates ID** (or passport plus visa).
2. **Original Mulkiya / vehicle registration document.** A photograph is
   not sufficient — the appraiser needs the original.
3. **Service book or service records** if available. The absence of a
   service book does not disqualify a trade-in but typically reduces the
   valuation by 3–5% because there is less certainty about maintenance
   history.
4. **Both sets of keys.** A missing second key typically reduces the offer
   by AED 500–1,500 depending on the vehicle. Key programming is not
   trivially cheap on newer cars.
5. **A copy of any outstanding finance agreement**, if the vehicle is
   still on finance.

## How valuation works

An **indicative range** is produced from four inputs:

1. **The vehicle's original list price.** Where the vehicle is in our
   internal catalogue, we use the recorded MSRP; where it is not, the
   appraiser uses a market comparable at inspection time.
2. **Age.** A vehicle loses roughly 20% of its value in the first year and
   continues to depreciate steeply for the first three years before the
   curve flattens.
3. **Recorded mileage.** Measured against an expectation of 15,000
   kilometres per year. Substantially below expectation raises the
   valuation; substantially above lowers it. Both directions are capped so
   an unusual odometer reading cannot dominate the estimate.
4. **Condition, as reported by the customer.** Self-reported condition is
   used to produce the indicative range; the *final* offer uses inspected
   condition.

**Renzo models hold their value somewhat better than Karva models**,
reflecting stronger residual demand in the used market for premium
vehicles. Performance-line Renzos have a smaller resale audience and can
carry a slightly higher variance — the appraiser flags this at inspection.

## Indicative, never binding

**Every figure quoted before physical inspection is an indicative range,
not an offer.** This is stated to the customer every time, without
exception.

The final offer is made only after the physical inspection and may differ
from the indicative range in either direction. A single precise number
before inspection reads as a commitment we have not made and cannot honour
before seeing the car.

The assistant on this platform quotes ranges and always includes the
disclaimer: *"This is an indicative range based on the details you
provided. The final offer is subject to a physical inspection at the
showroom."*

## When we decline to estimate

The automated estimate is **not produced** in four cases:

1. The vehicle is outside the accepted age range (20 years) or mileage
   range (400,000 km).
2. The brand or model is not in our reference catalogue and no comparable
   is on hand.
3. The customer has not supplied enough detail to identify the vehicle
   (missing model or year at minimum).
4. The vehicle has been substantially modified (aftermarket engine, body
   changes) — modifications are appraised only in person.

In all four cases the enquiry is passed to the Trade-In team for manual
appraisal. Handing off costs a phone call; a wrong number costs the sale
and the relationship.

## Inspection itself

The appraiser walks the vehicle in a defined order:

1. **Exterior walkaround** — bodywork, paint, panels, glass, lights.
2. **Interior** — seats, dashboard, electronics, air conditioning, sound
   system.
3. **Under the bonnet** — engine bay condition, fluid levels, belt and
   hose condition.
4. **Underbody** on a lift — suspension, exhaust, evidence of accident
   damage.
5. **A short drive** on the same route as our test drives.
6. **A settlement check with the previous finance provider**, if
   applicable.

A written appraisal is provided at the end. It lists any issues found and
the impact on the offer. The offer is valid for **7 days**.

## Outstanding finance

A vehicle with finance outstanding can still be traded in. The process:

1. The customer's current lender is contacted for a **settlement figure**
   (the amount required to close the loan today, including any early-settlement fee).
2. The settlement figure is deducted from the agreed trade-in value.
3. If the settlement exceeds the vehicle's value ("negative equity"), the
   difference can typically be rolled into the new financing agreement,
   subject to the new lender's approval.

Any enquiry involving outstanding finance is handled by the Finance team
alongside Trade-In. The assistant does not attempt to quote settlement
figures.

## Interaction with a new purchase

Where a customer is both trading in and financing a new vehicle:

- The trade-in value is **agreed first**.
- The part-exchange reduces the amount to be financed.
- The **monthly instalment is calculated on the reduced amount**, not on
  the full vehicle price.

This is why the assistant will always ask for trade-in details before
quoting an instalment when both intents are present in one message —
quoting the instalment first would give a number that has to be retracted
once the trade-in is valued.

## Response commitment

Trade-in enquiries receive a substantive response within **four hours**
during showroom hours. Where the enquiry is missing model, year or
mileage, we ask for the single most useful missing detail rather than
requesting everything at once.
