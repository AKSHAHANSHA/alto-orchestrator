# Brand guides

## Karva

Karva is Alto Motors' mass-market brand and the volume of the showroom.

**Who Karva is for:**
- First-time buyers upgrading from public transport or a used car.
- Families needing a sedan or a small-to-mid-size SUV.
- Expatriates who want reliable transport that retains value at resale.
- Drivers who prioritise fuel economy and running costs over performance.

**Where Karva sits:**
- **Price band**: AED 45,000 to AED 180,000 across the range.
- **Reliability**: strong. Karva vehicles are built for high-mileage,
  low-drama daily use.
- **Fuel economy**: consistently good in the segment — Karva sedans in
  particular are among the more efficient options at their price.
- **Warranty and service**: standard three-year manufacturer warranty,
  10,000 km service intervals.
- **Resale value**: solid. A three-year-old Karva typically retains 60 to
  65% of its original price with normal mileage and a full service book.

**What Karva does not do:**
- **Not a performance brand.** Karva makes efficient, comfortable cars —
  the top of the Karva range does not compete with a mid-range Renzo on
  acceleration or handling.
- **Not a luxury brand.** Interiors are practical and well-built rather
  than premium. Materials are cloth or synthetic leather across most of
  the range.
- **Not off-road specialists.** Karva SUVs are crossovers designed for
  paved-road use — some can handle mild dirt tracks, none are built for
  serious off-road driving.

### Karva model families in stock

The catalogue enumerates specific years and trims. In broad terms the Karva
range covers:

- **Sedan family** — compact and mid-size sedans, the volume line.
- **Crossover / small SUV family** — five-seat family cars with elevated
  driving position.
- **Mid-size SUV** — larger family car, seven-seat option available on
  some variants.
- **Hatchback** — one small hatchback for city driving and first-car
  buyers.
- **Coupe** — a small sporty two-door on the top end of the range.

For an exact stocked model, year, price and specification, the assistant
consults the catalogue. It does not invent models.

## Renzo

Renzo is Alto Motors' premium and performance brand.

**Who Renzo is for:**
- Returning customers looking to upgrade from Karva or from a competitor.
- Business owners who want the vehicle to reflect their business.
- Drivers who care about performance, handling and driving experience.
- Customers valuing luxury materials, cabin refinement and technology.

**Where Renzo sits:**
- **Price band**: AED 195,000 to AED 750,000+ across the range.
- **Performance**: strong across the range and exceptional at the top end.
  Renzo performance-line variants compete directly with the best in the
  segment.
- **Materials**: leather is standard across the range; premium leather,
  wood and metal trims on higher variants.
- **Warranty and service**: standard three-year manufacturer warranty,
  plus Renzo Roadside Assistance included for the warranty period.
- **Resale value**: stronger than Karva in absolute terms, though
  performance-line variants have a narrower resale audience and can
  carry higher variance.

**What Renzo does not do:**
- **Not budget transport.** Even the entry-level Renzo has a running-cost
  profile (fuel, insurance, service) noticeably higher than a comparable
  Karva.
- **Not the volume brand.** Some Renzo variants are ordered rather than
  stocked; delivery timelines are longer.
- **Not for every driver profile.** The Renzo performance line has age and
  licence-tenure requirements for test drives (see the test-drive
  procedure).

### Renzo model families in stock

- **Executive sedan family** — mid and large luxury sedans.
- **Luxury SUV family** — five and seven-seat premium SUVs.
- **Coupe and convertible** — two-door luxury and grand-tourer variants.
- **Performance line** — high-power sedans, coupes and a small number of
  factory-tuned variants that fall in the "Exotic" or "High-Performance"
  categories in the catalogue.

Performance-line variants require the driver to be at least 25 and to have
held a licence for three years for a test drive.

## Brand choice — how the assistant should frame it

When a customer is undecided between Karva and Renzo, the assistant does
not simply push toward the more expensive option. It surfaces the three
questions that actually decide the choice:

1. **Budget** — total, including insurance and running costs, not just
   the sticker price. A stretched-budget Renzo can be a worse experience
   than a comfortable-budget Karva.
2. **Use case** — daily commute, family transport, weekend driving,
   business use. Some Renzos are impractical as an only car for a
   commuting family; some Karvas are underwhelming as a business
   statement.
3. **Priorities** — reliability and cost of ownership vs performance and
   presence. Both are legitimate. Neither is objectively better.

The assistant never says "Renzo is better than Karva" or vice versa.
Better *for what* is the honest question.

## Cross-brand test drives

A customer considering both brands is welcome to test-drive one of each
in the same visit. Two 45-minute slots back-to-back, both booked in
advance, is the recommended arrangement — the sales team stages both
vehicles and walks the customer through the trade-offs directly.

Two test drives in one visit is a **strong buying signal**; the sales team
gives these visits extra preparation time.
