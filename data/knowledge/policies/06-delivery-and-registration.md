# Delivery and registration

> **Synthetic content.** Fictional dealer, patterned on UAE retail practice.

## Timeline from signing to delivery

For a vehicle **in stock** at the showroom:

- **Same-day handover** is possible if the customer pays cash or has
  pre-approved financing and their paperwork is complete.
- **48-hour handover** is typical when financing needs to be finalised —
  the bank issues the disbursement letter, we complete registration, and
  the vehicle is prepared for delivery.

For a vehicle **not in stock** — a specific colour, trim or variant that
we have to order:

- **Karva models**: 2 to 6 weeks depending on origin and availability.
- **Renzo models**: 4 to 12 weeks; performance and limited-edition variants
  can be longer and are quoted individually.

**We do not commit to a delivery date at the time of enquiry.** A specific
date is committed only after a sale agreement is signed and the vehicle's
location is confirmed. The assistant should offer a range, not a promise.

## Registration

All vehicles sold at Alto Motors are registered through the **Velmora
Road and Transport Authority (RTA)** — patterned on the UAE RTA process.
Registration is handled entirely by our delivery team; the customer does
not visit the RTA themselves.

Registration requires:

1. **The signed sale agreement**, listing the vehicle's VIN and the buyer's
   Emirates ID.
2. **Proof of insurance** for the vehicle — either from the bank's bundled
   insurance or the customer's own.
3. **Any bank documents required** — if the vehicle is on finance, the
   RTA records the bank's interest on the mortgage register.
4. **Payment of registration and Salik/toll-tag fees.**

The registration fee is a fixed cost regardless of vehicle price. The tag
fee is a one-off. Neither of these can be waived or discounted.

## Number plates

Plates come in three categories:

1. **Standard series** — the default. Assigned by the RTA sequentially,
   no premium.
2. **Preferred number** — customer chooses from an available list. Adds
   AED 3,500 to AED 25,000 depending on the number.
3. **Auction plates** — special series, requires attending or bidding in
   an RTA auction. We can arrange a bidder on the customer's behalf but
   the auction outcome is the customer's risk.

Category 1 is the vast majority of our deliveries. Categories 2 and 3 are
handled by a specialist on our delivery team.

## Plate transfer

A customer transferring their existing plate from an older vehicle
(including a trade-in) to the new one can do so during registration. The
transfer fee is AED 100 for a standard series, higher for preferred and
auction plates.

Plates cannot be transferred if there are unpaid fines or Salik charges on
the outgoing vehicle. The customer is asked to clear those before the
transfer. This check is quick and the assistant can flag it early in the
conversation.

## Temporary plates

If a customer needs to drive the vehicle before final registration — for
example, if they are collecting on a Friday morning before the RTA
office reopens on Saturday — we can arrange temporary plates through the
RTA's short-term registration service. Temporary plates are valid for up
to 7 days and cost AED 250.

Temporary plates are not something the assistant offers unprompted; they
are for genuine timing constraints and the delivery team decides on the
day.

## Insurance requirements at delivery

The vehicle cannot leave the showroom without a valid comprehensive
insurance certificate. Options:

1. **Bank-bundled**, if financed. Effective at delivery.
2. **Customer's own policy**, provided the customer presents the original
   certificate (not a screenshot) and the policy covers the vehicle's
   category. Category coverage for a Renzo performance model is stricter
   than for a standard Karva; the customer's broker must confirm
   coverage.

Third-party-only insurance is not accepted at delivery. Comprehensive is
mandatory for financed vehicles under CBUAE rules, and we require it for
cash-purchased Renzo vehicles as well.

## Vehicle preparation

Before delivery every vehicle receives:

- A final quality-control inspection.
- Interior and exterior detailing.
- A full tank of fuel.
- The service book and owner's manual, with warranty registered.
- Two sets of keys (three for Renzo — one main, one spare, one valet).
- Vehicle documents in a branded folder.

The customer takes delivery in the delivery bay at the rear of the
showroom, where a delivery specialist walks through the vehicle's
features, pairs the customer's phone, and takes the delivery photograph.
The walkthrough takes 30 to 45 minutes.

## Delivery to another emirate

The default is that the customer takes delivery at the showroom. Delivery
to another location in Velmora (or the analogue UAE emirate) is available
on request:

- **Same emirate**: complimentary, subject to a delivery specialist being
  free.
- **Other emirates**: possible but not automatic. Requires manager
  approval and a delivery fee of AED 400 to AED 1,200 depending on
  distance and vehicle value.

The assistant does not confirm off-emirate delivery — it always routes to
Sales for approval.

## Delivery outside Velmora

We do not deliver outside Velmora. Customers purchasing to export or to
drive across a land border arrange their own transport after taking
delivery at the showroom. We can help with export documentation on
request; a separate specialist handles this.

## The delivery day

We ask customers to arrive at their delivery appointment about 30 minutes
before the scheduled time to:

- Sign final paperwork.
- Meet the service advisor who will look after the vehicle going forward.
- Have the delivery photograph taken.
- Complete the feature walkthrough at their pace.

A delivery appointment typically takes 60 to 90 minutes. The customer
leaves with the vehicle, both sets of keys, all documents, and the
first-service voucher.
