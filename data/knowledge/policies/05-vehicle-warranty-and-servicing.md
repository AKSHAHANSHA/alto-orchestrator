# Vehicle warranty and servicing

> **Synthetic content.** Fictional dealer, patterned on UAE retail practice.

## New-vehicle warranty

Every new Karva and Renzo sold at Alto Motors comes with a
**manufacturer's warranty** covering three years or 100,000 kilometres,
whichever comes first. The clock starts on the day of first registration,
not the day of order.

The warranty covers:

- **Engine and drivetrain** — all lubricated components.
- **Electrical and electronic systems** — including the infotainment head
  unit, sensors and control modules.
- **Air conditioning system** — compressor, condenser, evaporator and
  associated electronics.
- **Paint and body panel corrosion**, subject to the standard exclusions
  below.
- **All factory-fitted equipment** — sunroofs, powered seats, driver-
  assistance systems.

The warranty does **not** cover:

- **Consumables**: tyres, brake pads and discs, wiper blades, filters,
  lubricants, batteries after the first 24 months.
- **Damage from misuse** — off-road driving in an on-road-only vehicle,
  towing beyond the rated capacity, running low on oil.
- **Damage from accidents** or from third-party modifications.
- **Cosmetic wear** from normal use.

Warranty claims are handled at the Alto Motors service centre. Warranty
work performed at another service centre is only reimbursed if the
customer had prior authorisation from Alto Motors and the work was
otherwise unavoidable (breakdown outside Velmora, for example).

## Renzo extended coverage

Renzo vehicles carry an additional **manufacturer roadside-assistance
programme** for the length of the warranty. The programme covers:

- **Recovery** to the nearest authorised service centre following a
  breakdown.
- **A replacement vehicle** for the duration of any warranty repair that
  takes longer than 24 hours.
- **Fuel delivery, tyre change and jump-start** anywhere in Velmora.

The Renzo assistance line runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and is
listed in the owner's manual and on the service-book cover.

## Extended-warranty options

Customers can extend their warranty at three points:

1. **At time of purchase.** Adds one or two years to the manufacturer's
   coverage. Cheapest option per year of extension.
2. **Before month 30.** The customer can add coverage before the
   manufacturer's warranty ends, at a modest premium over the
   time-of-purchase price.
3. **After the manufacturer warranty expires.** A separate third-party
   product, usually at a higher premium and with slightly narrower
   coverage.

Extended-warranty products are subject to a vehicle inspection at renewal
after the manufacturer warranty expires. Extended warranty is not a
substitute for a service schedule — a vehicle that has missed services
may be refused extended coverage.

## Service intervals

Both Karva and Renzo vehicles follow **10,000-kilometre or 12-month**
service intervals, whichever comes first. A vehicle driven fewer than
10,000 km per year still needs an annual service to preserve the warranty.

The service schedule alternates two types:

- **Minor service** (every 10,000 km): engine oil, oil filter, air filter,
  cabin filter, fluid top-ups, brake and tyre check, general inspection.
- **Major service** (every 20,000 km): everything in a minor service, plus
  spark plugs, fuel filter, transmission fluid check, air-conditioning
  service, brake fluid replacement on the 60,000 and 120,000 km services.

Major services take longer (typically 4–5 hours) and cost noticeably more.
The service book lists the exact items in each interval.

## First-service voucher

Every new-vehicle sale includes a **first-service voucher**. The voucher
covers the labour on the first minor service, up to and including the
20,000 km mark, provided the service is completed at the Alto Motors
service centre. The voucher does not cover parts.

The voucher is non-transferable and non-refundable. If the customer sells
the vehicle before using the voucher, it is void.

## Service pricing

Service pricing at Alto Motors is fixed by service package rather than by
line item. A published price list is available at the service reception
and on request through the platform. The assistant can retrieve pricing
for a specific service package but does not calculate custom quotes — a
service advisor does that at the desk.

Third-party service parts (from independent workshops) do not void the
manufacturer warranty in the UAE by law, but a warranty claim on a
component adjacent to third-party work is more likely to be scrutinised.
The safest path for a customer relying on warranty is to service with us
until the warranty expires.

## Booking a service

Service appointments can be booked through:

1. **The service reception**, in person or by phone during service centre
   hours.
2. **The platform**, subject to a service advisor confirming availability.
3. **Walk-in**, subject to bay availability. Walk-ins are accepted but a
   customer with an appointment gets priority.

The assistant does not confirm a specific service appointment — it holds a
provisional request and hands it to a service advisor for confirmation
within one business hour.

## Courtesy vehicle

While the vehicle is with the service centre, the customer may be offered
a courtesy vehicle:

- **For warranty repairs** taking longer than 24 hours: yes, subject to
  courtesy-vehicle availability.
- **For scheduled service** taking longer than half a day: sometimes,
  subject to availability and only for premium (Renzo) customers.
- **For customer-paid repairs** on their own vehicle: at the service
  advisor's discretion; typically a small charge applies.

Courtesy vehicles are always Karva models regardless of the customer's
own vehicle, and always with a full tank at pickup — the customer returns
it with a full tank.

## Recall notifications

Recalls affecting Karva and Renzo vehicles are notified by SMS and email
to the registered owner. Recall work is always free and always takes
priority over normal service bookings.

The assistant can look up whether a specific vehicle (by VIN or plate) has
an open recall, but this lookup requires the customer to identify the
vehicle unambiguously. The recall database itself is not something the
assistant guesses at.
