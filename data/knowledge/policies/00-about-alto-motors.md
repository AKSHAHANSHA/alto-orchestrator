# About Alto Motors

> **Synthetic content.** Alto Motors is a fictional dealership operating in
> the fictional market of Velmora. The procedures below are patterned on UAE
> retail-auto practice for realism, but no real dealer, bank or authority is
> being described. This document exists so the customer-support assistant has
> reliable dealer-specific evidence to answer from.

## Who we are

Alto Motors is Velmora's exclusive dealer for two brands: **Karva** and
**Renzo**. We are a single-showroom dealership serving individual customers
and small businesses. We do not sell fleet vehicles, and we do not deal in
used cars from brands we do not represent — we only take trade-ins.

We have been operating from the same address for eleven years. The showroom
and the service centre share a site; sales, finance, trade-in, service and
customer relations all sit under one roof, which is what lets us take a
customer from *"I might be interested"* to *"here are the keys"* without
handing them between three different companies.

## What we sell

**Karva** is the mass-market brand. Sedans, small and mid-size SUVs, one
crossover and one small hatchback. Prices sit between AED 45,000 and AED
180,000. Karva is the volume of the showroom floor: most enquiries, most
trade-ins, most weekend test drives. It appeals to first-time buyers,
families upgrading from a smaller car, and expatriates who want reliable
transport that keeps its value.

**Renzo** is the premium and performance brand. Executive sedans, luxury
SUVs, coupes, convertibles and a performance line. Prices start at AED
195,000 and reach well above AED 700,000 for the flagship performance models.
Renzo customers are typically returning buyers, business owners and drivers
who care about performance, materials and residual value. Renzo has stricter
test-drive rules (see the test-drive procedure document) and more
sophisticated financing conversations.

We do not stock every variant of every model. The vehicle catalogue exposed
in this platform shows what we have listed; a customer who names a model we
do not stock is told so directly and offered the nearest equivalent.

## What we do not do

- **We do not sell cars we do not represent.** If a customer asks about a
  brand that is not Karva or Renzo, refer them politely and do not attempt
  to compare.
- **We do not sell used cars from other brands.** Trade-ins we accept get
  auctioned onward; we do not retail them.
- **We do not lease vehicles.** Financing, salary-transfer terms and cash
  purchase only.
- **We do not deliver to other emirates without a formal request.** The
  customer visits the showroom for handover, or arranges their own
  transport.

## Who's on the team

Customer-facing:
- **Sales consultants** — first point of contact, guide through models,
  arrange test drives.
- **Finance consultants** — instalment structures, bank partners,
  paperwork.
- **Trade-in appraisers** — inspection and valuation of vehicles being
  traded.
- **Service advisors** — after-sales, maintenance, warranty.
- **Customer relations** — complaints, escalations, general enquiries.

The AI assistant on this platform routes to those five teams. It does not
attempt to answer regulatory or medical questions, and it never quotes a
final finance figure without a bank sign-off.

## Values that shape our replies

The style guide the AI assistant follows:

1. **Warm but not effusive.** We greet, we answer, we do not overdo it.
2. **Specific over general.** A range of prices, a named model, an actual
   day. Vagueness reads as evasion.
3. **Own the limits.** If we do not stock a model, we say so and offer
   alternatives. If a figure needs a bank's sign-off, we say that too.
4. **Bilingual when the customer is.** If any Arabic appears in the
   message, the reply carries both languages.
5. **Handover, not handoff.** When a conversation reaches a person, it
   stays with a person — automation does not quietly resume.
