# After-sales and complaints

> **Synthetic content.** Fictional dealer, patterned on UAE retail practice.

## After-sales support in one sentence

Everything after a customer drives their car out of the showroom — the
service centre, the warranty, roadside assistance, complaints, and the
customer relations team who owns the relationship long-term — is handled
under one roof at the same address as the showroom.

## Service centre

Bookings, service intervals, warranty work, courtesy vehicles and recall
handling are covered in `05-vehicle-warranty-and-servicing.md`. In brief:

- **Service centre hours**: Monday to Saturday, closed on Sunday.
  Ramadan hours differ; see `01-showroom-hours-and-visits.md`.
- **Booking**: through the service reception, the platform, or as a
  walk-in. Appointments get priority.
- **Warranty claims**: three-year manufacturer warranty on every new
  vehicle, honoured directly by the service centre.

## Roadside assistance

**Renzo** vehicles under warranty carry manufacturer roadside assistance
24/7, listed in the service book. Coverage: recovery, replacement
vehicle after 24-hour repairs, fuel delivery, tyre change, jump-start.

**Karva** vehicles are not enrolled in a manufacturer programme by default,
but the customer can add an Alto Motors Roadside Care membership for AED
450 per year. Coverage is functionally similar to the Renzo programme.

Roadside assistance is not something the assistant dispatches — it is a
phone call to the number in the customer's service book or membership
card. The assistant can look up the number and can hand a customer to
customer relations if the assistance provider itself has let them down.

## Complaints — the customer's rights

A customer with a complaint has three routes, in order of speed:

1. **Talk to the department involved.** The service advisor, the finance
   consultant, or the sales consultant who handled the sale. Most
   complaints are resolved at this level within one working day.
2. **Escalate to customer relations.** If the department cannot resolve
   it, or if the customer prefers, customer relations owns the complaint
   end-to-end. Customer relations commits to a **first response within 24
   hours** and to a **resolution proposal within five working days**.
3. **External bodies.** For finance-related complaints, the CBUAE and
   the customer's financing bank both have their own complaint routes.
   For service and warranty complaints, the manufacturer's regional
   customer service is an option. Customer relations will provide the
   contact details on request.

**No complaint is answered by the AI assistant.** Every complaint is
routed to customer relations immediately. This is a hard rule — the
assistant does not attempt to placate, does not try to defend a decision,
and does not delay handoff.

## What a good complaint response looks like

The customer relations team follows a written playbook:

1. **Acknowledge the customer's experience** in the first response — not
   the same as agreeing with their conclusion.
2. **Investigate before proposing.** No settlement is offered until the
   sales, service or finance record has been reviewed.
3. **Propose a specific resolution.** "We will do X by Y date" beats
   "we will look into it."
4. **Follow up in writing** whatever was agreed verbally.
5. **Close the loop.** A complaint is not closed until the customer
   confirms they are satisfied with the resolution.

## Escalation triggers

Some conversations are escalated to customer relations regardless of what
the customer literally said. The assistant on this platform applies these
hard overrides:

- **Any use of the word "complaint", "unacceptable", "refund", "manager"
  or similar** — routed to customer relations even if the message
  otherwise reads as a routine enquiry.
- **Negative sentiment combined with urgency** — an angry customer
  reaches a person; automation does not attempt to handle a person's
  frustration, ever.
- **Any indication the customer feels misled** — routed with the full
  transcript so customer relations can see what was said and by which
  channel.

The AI never argues with an angry customer, and never sends a first
response to a complaint. If a complaint comes in outside customer
relations' hours, it queues for first thing the following morning and the
customer receives an acknowledgement that a person will pick it up.

## Common issues and how they are handled

**Vehicle collected late from service.**
Customer relations checks whether a delay was communicated. If not, the
service is discounted by 20% and the customer is offered a complimentary
courtesy vehicle at next service.

**A quote given in enquiry differed from the final invoice.**
A common source of complaint. Customer relations investigates the quote's
source (assistant, consultant, or third party) and either honours it,
explains the difference, or refunds. Explicit assistant-quoted numbers
carrying a disclaimer are not treated as commitments.

**Finance approval rejected after the customer expected acceptance.**
Not our decision; it's the bank's. Customer relations helps the customer
understand what to try next: another bank in our panel, a co-applicant,
a larger down payment. We do not misrepresent this as our decision.

**A test drive was rescheduled at short notice.**
The customer is offered the next available slot with priority and is
called personally by the sales manager.

**A recall was performed but did not fix the issue the customer reported.**
Escalated to the service centre manager, who liaises with the
manufacturer's field engineer if needed.

## What customer relations cannot do

- **Reverse a bank's finance decision.** The bank's approval or rejection
  is not something we can override.
- **Retract regulatory constraints.** A customer who wants an 80-month
  tenure cannot have it; the regulator forbids it.
- **Change a vehicle's specification after the fact.** A trim ordered
  cannot be re-specified — only cancelled or replaced.

We are honest about these limits at the first response, not after three
weeks of investigation.

## Feedback vs complaint

Not every negative message is a complaint. Some customers write to say
what they would have preferred, without expecting resolution. Customer
relations logs these as feedback, thanks the customer, and — if it
affects a future purchase decision — flags it for the sales team.

The assistant does not distinguish feedback from complaint. It routes
anything with a negative tone to customer relations, and the team makes
the call at the first response.
